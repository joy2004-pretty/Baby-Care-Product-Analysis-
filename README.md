# Baby Care Products Market Performance Analysis (2022–2023)
Understanding Market Dynamics through Detailed Product Analysis of the Baby Care Segment.
## 1. Project Overview

This project involved cleaning, transforming and analysing a baby-care products dataset using Microsoft Excel to evaluate market performance and identify manufacturer growth trends.

The analysis focused on comparing sales value and sales volume between 2022 and 2023, examining manufacturer performance, and identifying manufacturers with the highest year-on-year sales volume growth.

The project demonstrates the use of Excel for data preparation, exploratory analysis, performance measurement and reporting.

## 2. Project Objectives

The main objectives were to:

- Transform the original messy dataset into a clean, structured and analysis-ready dataset.
- Analyse overall market performance using sales value and sales volume.
- Compare performance between 2022 and 2023.
- Calculate year-on-year growth rates.
- Identify the top three manufacturers with the highest percentage growth in sales volume.
- Present the findings in a structured performance report.

## 3. Tools and Techniques

**Tool:** Microsoft Excel

**Techniques applied:**
- Data cleaning and transformation.
- PivotTables for data summarisation and analysis.
- Calculated fields and Excel formulas.
- Year-on-year growth calculations.
- Sorting and ranking manufacturer performance.
- Performance reporting.

## 4. Project Workflow

The project followed these stages:

**Stage 1: Data Preparation**

The original dataset contained unstructured data that required cleaning and organisation before analysis.

The dataset was transformed into a structured format, with clearly organised fields suitable for further analysis.

**Stage 2: Exploratory Data Analysis**

PivotTables were used to summarise sales performance across manufacturers and brands.

The analysis focused on:
- Total sales value in 2022 and 2023.
- Total sales volume in 2022 and 2023.
- Manufacturer-level sales volume.
- Year-on-year performance changes.

**Stage 3: Performance Measurement**

Calculated fields and formulas were used to measure year-on-year growth.

The growth calculation was:

`Growth Rate = ((2023 Value - 2022 Value) / 2022 Value) × 100`

This was applied to evaluate changes in sales value and sales volume.

**Stage 4: Reporting**

The results were consolidated into a performance report highlighting overall market performance and manufacturer growth.

## 5. Key Findings

The analysis revealed the following changes between 2022 and 2023:

| Performance indicator | 2022 | 2023 | Growth |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Sales value (USD '000) | 11,880.16 | 12,264.85 | +3.24% |
| Sales volume ('000 units) | 470,247.93 | 475,047.06 | +1.02% |

### Top Three Manufacturers by Sales Volume Growth

The following manufacturers recorded the highest positive year-on-year percentage growth in sales volume:

| Rank | Manufacturer | Growth rate |
|---:|---|---:|
| 1 | OnTop | 4,788.21% |
| 2 | ErusHealth Products | 959.94% |
| 3 | PureAim Cleaners | 302.37% |

**Additional observation:**

OnTop recorded the highest percentage growth, increasing from 0.3384 thousand units in 2022 to 16.5417 thousand units in 2023.

Although the percentage growth was exceptionally high, the starting sales volume was relatively small. This highlights the importance of considering both percentage growth and absolute sales volume when evaluating manufacturer performance.

## 6. Business Insights

The findings suggest that:

- The overall market experienced positive growth in both sales value and sales volume.
- Sales value grew faster than sales volume during the analysis period.
- Manufacturer performance varied, with some manufacturers recording substantial growth while others experienced declines.
- Percentage growth should be interpreted alongside the underlying sales figures to avoid misleading conclusions.

## 8. Limitations and Considerations

- The analysis focuses on the comparison between 2022 and 2023.
- YTD 2024 data is available in the dataset but is not included in the main year-on-year comparison.
- Manufacturers with zero or missing 2022 sales volume require special consideration because their percentage growth cannot be calculated using the standard growth formula.
- High percentage growth does not necessarily indicate the highest absolute sales volume.
